### Patterns
- Full GoldenKit gate in CI (#29) plus realistic and licensed public samples (#41-#43) caught real cross-platform bugs: OS newline in MimeKit output and zip headers (#62), and OS newline in nested .eml attachment bytes (#99, PartCollector fix).

### Rule suggestions
- Generators and serializers must force CRLF / fixed metadata; never depend on Environment.NewLine.
- Golden generation should be deterministic (fixed MIME boundaries).
- Add a Q-encoded filename carrier test (gap noted in #97).

Quality evidence epic delivered a real CI gate and cross-platform samples; it paid for itself by exposing two platform-dependent byte bugs.
